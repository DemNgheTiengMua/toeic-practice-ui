## What to build

Real-time persistence and crash recovery infrastructure (`AttemptEngine`, `ExamsController`, `Take.cshtml`) that eliminates data loss during exam sittings across all 11 question components.

Candidate responses—including single-choice `SelectedOptionId`, multi-select `SelectedOptionIdsJson`, and completion/matching/essay `ResponseText`—along with tokenized passage highlights/notes (`CandidateNotesJson`) and active audio offset (`LastAudioOffsetMs`) are debounced to 500ms and synchronized automatically in the background. If network connectivity drops, responses buffer locally in the browser and automatically flush to the server upon reconnection. If a computer shuts down, crashes, or the tab is closed, the candidate can return, enter their `AuthorizationCode`, and resume their attempt at the exact active skill, unit, question, audio offset, and remaining wall-clock time.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Client responses across all 11 question components (`SelectedOptionId`, `SelectedOptionIdsJson`, `ResponseText`) and candidate highlights/notes synchronize automatically to the server with 500ms debounce.
- [ ] Network disconnects trigger an offline alert banner ("Bảng hiệu phòng thi" Sign Gold/Red style) and buffer unsynced answers locally until reconnected.
- [ ] A dedicated resume flow accepts an attempt `AuthorizationCode` and validates that the attempt is still unexpired.
- [ ] Resuming restores the candidate's active skill, active unit/group, last question position, audio offset (`LastAudioOffsetMs`), tokenized highlights/notes, and all recorded answers.
- [ ] A `beforeunload` browser alert prevents accidental tab closure while an exam sitting is in progress.
