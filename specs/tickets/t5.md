## What to build

The **Split-Screen & Sandbox Sitting Workspace** (`Take.cshtml` and modular sitting JS/CSS) conforming to the "Bảng hiệu phòng thi" design system (`DESIGN.md`: flat 2px ink borders, 0 border radius, no box shadows), driven dynamically by the section blueprint's `LayoutType = SPLIT_HORIZONTAL`, `NavigationMode = FULL_SANDBOX`, `ToolsConfigJson`, and the **11 interactive question component widgets**.

Powers IELTS on Computer (Listening & Reading), VSTEP Reading, TOEIC Part 6/7 split passages, and any custom certificate configured with split-screen or sandbox navigation:
1. **Split-Screen Viewport & Top Control Bar:**
   - 100vh viewport lock with independent scrollbars for the reading passage / stimulus (left pane) and question groups (right pane) whenever `LayoutType = SPLIT_HORIZONTAL`.
   - Top control bar with authoritative countdown timer that flashes Sign Red (`#b31217`) with an alert banner at configured warning thresholds (`warnAtSec`: `[600, 300]` = 10 minutes and 5 minutes remaining), audio volume slider, and an Accessibility Modal allowing real-time switching of font sizes (`Standard`, `Large`, `Extra Large`) and high-contrast color schemes (`Black on White`, `White on Black`, `Yellow on Black`) without page reloads.
2. **11 Interactive Question Component Widgets & Bottom Question Palette:**
   - Renders interactive candidate controls for `mcq` (4 options), `mcq_3` (3 options), `multi_select` (checkboxes enforcing `MaxSelections`), `inline_completion` (inline text/table inputs displaying `MaxWords` / `AllowNumber` badges), `dropdown` (matching select), and `drag_drop` (token bank to drop-zone placement).
   - Bottom question palette reflecting `Unanswered`, `Answered`, `Current` (inverted solid ink `#14181d`), and `Flagged` (Sign Gold `#f2b705`) states, allowing free navigation across questions during `FULL_SANDBOX` Listening without interrupting active audio playback.
3. **Tokenized Text Highlighter & Notes Engine (`ToolsConfigJson.allowHighlight` / `allowNotes`):**
   - When enabled by the section's `ToolsConfigJson`, right-clicking selected text opens a context menu for `[Highlight]` and `[Notes]` anchored to immutable word-token indices (`{ start, end }`) that never drift across font size or contrast theme changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sections with `LayoutType = SPLIT_HORIZONTAL` render in a 100vh vertical split-screen with independent left/right scroll areas.
- [ ] Top control bar displays countdown timer, volume slider, and accessibility modal for live font scaling and high-contrast switching; timer flashes red with a banner at 10:00 and 05:00 remaining (`warnAtSec`).
- [ ] Candidate UI widgets render and capture responses for `mcq`, `mcq_3`, `multi_select` (enforcing `MaxSelections`), `inline_completion`, `dropdown`, and `drag_drop`.
- [ ] Bottom question palette updates across all 4 states (`unanswered`, `answered`, `current`, `flagged`) and allows looking ahead in `FULL_SANDBOX` Listening without interrupting audio playback.
- [ ] When `allowHighlight` / `allowNotes` are enabled in `ToolsConfigJson`, text selection and right-click context menu create tokenized `{ start, end }` highlights and sticky notes that remain stable across font/theme changes.
