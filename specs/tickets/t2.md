## What to build

A mandatory pre-flight onboarding gate (`Preflight.cshtml`, `AttemptEngine`) that runs before a candidate can enter any exam sitting whose Certificate blueprint includes Listening audio (`RequireAudio = true`) or Speaking recording (`SkillCode = 'speaking'`).

Frontend pre-flight fetches and buffers all exam audio assets upfront and verifies network readiness. If any audio stream fails to load, the exam start is safely blocked with a retry prompt, eliminating buffering failures mid-exam. Whenever the exam includes a Speaking skill, candidates are guided through a microphone positioning guide, record a short sample sentence, play it back, and confirm audio clarity (Yes/No) before the "Start Test" button unlocks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Candidates entering any exam whose blueprint contains audio assets or a Speaking skill are routed to the pre-flight verification screen before the attempt timer starts.
- [ ] The pre-flight interface fetches and buffers 100% of the audio resources for the exam; failures display an actionable retry prompt and block proceeding.
- [ ] Speaking exams present an interactive microphone check workflow: record audio sample, playback, and choose Yes (proceed) or No (re-test).
- [ ] The "Start Test" button remains disabled until required audio buffering and microphone verification pass successfully.
- [ ] Automated tests verify that unverified candidates cannot bypass pre-flight to start the exam sitting.
