## Execution Order

**Defect remediation 5 of 8 — Palette perceived as broken**

## What to build

Locked question chips in the bottom palette and the sidebar navigator say why they are locked in a way assistive technology can read, and questions inside the audio group the candidate is currently working through stay reachable. The palette then reads as deliberately gated rather than broken.

## Why it broke

Reported as 'question palette does not work'. Locked chips are disabled without any explanation, and within the same audio cluster a candidate sitting on Q3/Q4 cannot re-enter Q1/Q2 even though the cluster is meant to be revisitable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every locked chip and navigator cell exposes an accessible reason for being locked, not hover-only text.
- [ ] The palette legend documents the locked state alongside unanswered, answered, current and flagged.
- [ ] Questions that belong to the same question group as the current question remain enterable.
- [ ] Questions behind an irreversible unit lock or in a future skill remain locked.
- [ ] Attempting to activate a locked chip produces no navigation and no console error.
- [ ] WebMVC test asserts a locked navigator entry is rendered with an accessible explanation.
