## Execution Order

**Defect remediation 3 of 8 — Blocking dead-end in TOEIC Speaking & Writing**

## What to build

Continuing from the Speaking audio verification screen either reaches the Writing section or tells the candidate why it did not. A successful transition hides the overlay and renders the next skill's first question with no client-side phase recalculation undoing it. A failed transition leaves the candidate on a screen that shows the server's reason and offers a retry, and does not consume the attempt.

## Why it broke

Reproduced on `TOEIC Speaking & Writing Mock Test 1` (exam 18): the continue control fires the transition without inspecting the response, so a 400 leaves the candidate on a dead overlay with Questions 2 and 3 unreachable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Continuing from the verification screen displays the first Writing question and hides the verification overlay.
- [ ] The displayed question matches the skill and group the transition response reports.
- [ ] A non-success response surfaces the server's message inline and offers a retry action; the overlay stays usable.
- [ ] A retry after a transient failure succeeds once the server accepts the transition.
- [ ] A transition rejected because the attempt is no longer active is reported distinctly from a transient failure, so the candidate knows the attempt cannot be resumed.
- [ ] No response body is discarded: the failure path distinguishes "not in progress" from "wrong phase".
- [ ] E2E test drives a `SYSTEM_PACED_AUTO` question to the verification screen, continues, and asserts the Writing question is rendered.
