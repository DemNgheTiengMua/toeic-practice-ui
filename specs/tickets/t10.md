## What to build

Steps 1 to 3 of the non-technical **Linear Guided Certificate Wizard** and the **Official Blueprint Preset** engine (`CertificateService`, `CertificatesController`, `Views/Admin/CertificateWizard.cshtml`) that translates the 6 underlying exam rule axes and section authoring constraints into plain, human-readable choices.

Designed specifically for non-technical educators and administrators following the "Bảng hiệu phòng thi" (`DESIGN.md`) aesthetic:
1. **Step 1 — Pick a Starting Blueprint Preset:** Visual cards for **IELTS (Academic/GT)**, **TOEIC Listening & Reading (Standard CBT)**, **TOEIC Listening & Reading (Adaptive CAT)**, **TOEIC Speaking & Writing**, **VSTEP (B1–B2–C1)**, or **Blank Custom Certificate**. Selecting a preset pre-populates 100% of skills, sections, expected question counts, option counts, cluster sizes, allowed question types, timing rules, layout rules, and candidate tools in one click.
2. **Step 2 — Structure Skills & Timing:** Reorder skills (macro-linearity) and select plain-language timing rules per skill (*"Shared timer for the whole skill"*, *"Audio playback duration + extra review countdown"*, or *"Automated per-question prep & recording timer"*).
3. **Step 3 — Section Behavior, Layout, Authoring Blueprint & Candidate Tools:** Plain-language toggle switches per section (zero JSON editing) that govern **both the candidate sitting room AND the downstream Exam Studio / Nạp đề authoring rules**:
   - **Navigation behavior (`NavigationMode`):** *"Free back & forth navigation"*, *"One-way synced to audio (no going back)"*, *"Two-stage adaptive units (lock Unit 1 after advancing to Unit 2)"*, *"Auto-paced prep ➔ beep ➔ record"*, or *"Shared time pool for early items + lock confirmation on essays"*.
   - **Screen layout, stimulus & authoring slot rules:** *"Full-width single column"* vs *"Vertical split-screen (Passage on left, Questions on right)"*, *"Hide option text during listening"*, *"Group N questions per cluster (e.g. 3 for Part 3 & 4)"*, default option count (`3` for A–C, `4` for A–D), adaptive unit count (`1` or `2`), required stimuli (`RequirePassage`, `RequireAudio`, `RequireImage`), mandatory prompt keyword count (`RequiredKeywordCount`, e.g., `2` for TOEIC Writing Q1–5), and allowed question component types (`AllowedComponentTypesJson` from the 11 supported components).
   - **Allowed candidate tools (`ToolsConfigJson`):** Checkboxes for *"Text Highlighter & Notes"*, *"Live Word Counter & Disable Browser Spellcheck"*, and *"Block keyboard Copy/Paste (require on-screen Cut/Paste buttons)"*.
