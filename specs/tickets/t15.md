## What to build

**Certificate-Driven, Section-Aware Quick Ingest** (`ExamQuickIngestService`, `_QuickIngestModal.cshtml`) and **AI Section/Exam Generator** (`ExamGeneratorService`) integrated directly into the Section-Tab Guided Exam Studio.

Because each Section Tab in Exam Studio dynamically reads its Certificate's persisted section blueprint (#116 — `ExpectedQuestionCount`, `OptionCount`, `ClusterSize`, `AllowedComponentTypesJson`, `RequirePassage`, `RequireAudio`, `RequireImage`, `RequiredKeywordCount`), bulk authoring inside any tab adapts automatically to the active Certificate and Section:
1. **Certificate-Driven, Section-Aware Quick Ingest Modal:**
   - When opened from a specific Section Tab, the modal inspects that section's blueprint from the Certificate definition and displays a **plain-language copy-paste guide and 1-click sample template** tailored to that section's rules:
     - *Audio-Only Answer Key Mode (`RequirePassage = false`, `AllowedComponentTypes = mcq/mcq_3`):* Paste a simple list of answer keys (`1. A`, `2. C`, `3. B`...) and optional transcripts to populate all `ExpectedQuestionCount` slots in seconds, enforcing `OptionCount` (`3` vs `4`).
     - *Clustered & Passage Mode (`ClusterSize > 1` or `RequirePassage = true`):* Paste passages and question blocks supporting the section's allowed components: completion answers (`ANSWER: library | the library`, `MAX_WORDS: 2`), multi-select keys (`ANSWER: A, C`), and dropdown/drag-and-drop mappings.
     - *Keyword Free-Response Mode (`RequiredKeywordCount > 0`):* Paste prompts and required keyword sets (`KEYWORDS: photograph, yesterday`) validated against `RequiredKeywordCount`.
   - Shows a live visual preview table with row-by-row green/red validation badges checked against the Certificate's section blueprint before committing into the current Section Tab's slots.
2. **Certificate-Driven AI Section Fill & Full Exam Generator (`ExamGeneratorService`):**
   - Allows educators to click **"AI Fill Missing Slots in This Section"** inside any Section Tab (or generate a full exam), dynamically building the Claude prompt and JSON schema from that Certificate section's `ExpectedQuestionCount`, `OptionCount`, `ClusterSize`, `RequiredKeywordCount`, and `AllowedComponentTypesJson`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `_QuickIngestModal.cshtml` dynamically adapts its instructions, placeholder examples, and parser rules to the active Section Tab's Certificate blueprint (#116)—working seamlessly for both official presets and custom certificates.
- [ ] `ExamQuickIngestService` parses plain-text and JSON inputs for rapid answer-key-only entry, clustered questions, inline completion (`ANSWER: a | b`, `MAX_WORDS: N`), multi-select (`ANSWER: A, C`), and mandatory prompt keywords (`KEYWORDS: w1, w2`), validating every item against the section's Certificate blueprint.
- [ ] Live preview in the Quick Ingest modal highlights any validation mismatch against the Certificate section's rules (e.g., option `D` pasted into an `OptionCount = 3` section, or disallowed question component type) before commit.
- [ ] `ExamGeneratorService` dynamically constructs its generation constraints from the Certificate's section blueprint to fill a specific section or full exam conforming to all 11 question component schemas.
- [ ] Unit and integration tests in `ExamQuickIngestServiceTests` and `GenerativeExamServiceTests.cs` verify parsing, blueprint validation, and AI generation across IELTS, TOEIC, VSTEP, and custom certificate section formats.
