## What to build

A **Certificate-Driven, Section-Tab Guided Exam Studio** (`ExamStudio.cshtml`, `ExamEditor.cshtml`, `AdminExamService`) that dynamically queries the chosen Certificate's persisted 6-axis section blueprint (#116) and automatically scaffolds a mistake-proof, guided authoring workspace across all skills, sections, and 11 interactive question components.

**Strict Certificate Compliance Invariant:** Exam Studio never hardcodes `IF CertCode == "TOEIC"` or `"IELTS"`. Instead, it dynamically adapts its tabs, slots, form fields, allowed question types, and publish validation strictly to whatever the chosen Certificate configured in the Certificate Wizard (#116)—meaning even a brand-new custom certificate immediately gets a tailored linear authoring guide:
1. **Auto-Scaffolded Skill & Section Tabs (Free Tab Switching + Certificate-Driven Linear Section Guide):**
   - Upon creating an exam and picking a Certificate & Delivery Mode (`strict_exam` or `practice`), Exam Studio reads the Certificate's `Skills` and `Sections` blueprints to generate top-level **Skill Tabs** and nested **Section/Part Tabs** with live completion counters (e.g., `Part 1 (6/6 ✓)`, `Part 2 (10/25)`).
   - Authors can switch freely between any Skill or Section tab in any order, while inside each tab the interface acts as a **smart linear guide** driven strictly by that Section's persisted blueprint flags:
     - **When Section Blueprint specifies `RequireImage` / `RequireAudio` / `OptionCount = 3` (e.g. Photo & Audio-Only Sections like TOEIC Part 1 & Part 2):** Auto-generates `ExpectedQuestionCount` slots; shows Photo Upload when `RequireImage = true`, locks option inputs to `OptionCount` (`3` for A–C or `4` for A–D), and hides passage editors when `RequirePassage = false`.
     - **When Section Blueprint specifies `ClusterSize > 1` (e.g. Clustered Audio Sections like TOEIC Part 3 & Part 4):** Auto-scaffolds `ClusterSize`-question cluster cards paired with audio slice timestamp controls (`AudioStartMs` – `AudioEndMs`).
     - **When Section Blueprint specifies `LayoutType = SPLIT_HORIZONTAL` or `RequirePassage = true` (e.g. IELTS Reading, TOEIC Part 6/7, VSTEP Reading):** Presents a guided step 1 (*"Paste Reading Passage / Stimulus"*) on the left and step 2 (*"Add Question Group"*) on the right, filtering the question type picker strictly to the section's `AllowedComponentTypesJson` (from the 11 interactive question components: Single Choice 4/3, Multi-Select Checkbox with `MaxSelections`, Inline Text Completion with `AcceptedAnswers`, `MaxWords`, and `AllowNumber`, Dropdown Matching, and Drag-and-Drop Labelling).
     - **When Section Blueprint specifies `NavigationMode = UNIT_LOCKED_SANDBOX` / `UnitCount > 1` (e.g. Adaptive CAT Sections):** Clearly segments the section tab into **Unit 1 (Stage 1)** and **Unit 2 (Adaptive Stage 2)** slots.
     - **When Section Blueprint specifies `AllowedComponentTypes = free_response` & `RequiredKeywordCount > 0` (e.g. TOEIC Writing Q1–5, IELTS/VSTEP Writing & Speaking):** Pre-builds task slots with mandatory input boxes for the exact `RequiredKeywordCount` (e.g., `[Required Keyword 1]` & `[Required Keyword 2]`) and word/timing thresholds (`MinWords`, `PrepSeconds`, `ResponseSeconds`).
2. **Certificate-Driven Pre-Publish Readiness Checklist:**
   - Displays a real-time checklist banner/drawer evaluating every section against its Certificate blueprint (`ExpectedQuestionCount`, `OptionCount`, `ClusterSize`, `AllowedComponentTypesJson`, `RequirePassage`, `RequireAudio`, `RequireImage`, `RequiredKeywordCount`), showing green `[✓]` completed sections and clickable `[!]` missing requirements that jump directly to the target tab and slot.
   - `PublishExamAsync` and `AuditExamPublishReadinessAsync` enforce the chosen Certificate's section blueprint rules and unlock publication once all checklist items are green.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Creating an exam dynamically reads the selected Certificate's persisted section blueprints (#116) to scaffold Skill/Section tabs and expected question/cluster slots—working identically for official presets (IELTS, TOEIC, VSTEP) and custom certificates without hardcoded `CertCode` branches.
- [ ] Authors can switch freely across Skill and Section tabs while each section renders a context-aware guided form driven by that section's blueprint (`OptionCount`, `ClusterSize`, `LayoutType`, `AllowedComponentTypesJson`, `RequirePassage`, `RequireAudio`, `RequireImage`, `RequiredKeywordCount`, `UnitCount`).
- [ ] Exam Studio supports authoring and previewing all 11 question components (`mcq`, `mcq_3`, `multi_select`, `inline_completion`, `dropdown`, `drag_drop`, `free_response`), restricted per section to `AllowedComponentTypesJson`.
- [ ] Modifying a Certificate's section blueprint in the Certificate Wizard (#116) is immediately reflected in Exam Studio's tabs, slot counts, allowed components, and readiness checklist for draft exams of that certificate.
- [ ] The Interactive Pre-Publish Readiness Checklist highlights incomplete slots per section, jumps to the target question on click, and gates `PublishExamAsync` until all Certificate blueprint rules pass.
- [ ] Integration tests in `AdminExamServiceTests` verify auto-scaffolding, 11-component authoring, and publish auditing across IELTS, TOEIC, VSTEP, and a custom-configured certificate.
