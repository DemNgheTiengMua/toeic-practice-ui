CARD 1: THE RULE 1 / 4 cards viewed

Rule: Present Continuous for Ongoing Actions

The present continuous describes actions happening right now, at the moment of speaking, or around the current time period.

Subject + is / am / are + Verb-ing

Signals in TOEIC Part 5: now, currently, presently, at the moment, this week.

Common Pitfalls & Exam Traps

Watch out for stative verbs that NEVER appear in the continuous aspect on the TOEIC:

TRAP 1: Stative Verbs of Perception & Cognition
WRONG: "He is knowing the answer." → CORRECT: "He knows the answer." (know, understand, believe, remember).
TRAP 2: Possession Verbs
WRONG: "The firm is owning five branches." → CORRECT: "The firm owns five branches." (own, have, belong).
TRAP 3: Missing Auxiliary 'Be'
WRONG: "The committee reviewing the draft now." → CORRECT: "The committee is reviewing the draft now."
PART 5 WORKED EXAMPLE

Recognizing the Aspect in Context

"Mr. Tanaka currently ------- the quarterly budget with regional supervisors in Osaka."
(A) reviews — Wrong: simple present does not match temporary 'currently' modifier
(B) is reviewing — Correct: singular subject 'Mr. Tanaka' + 'currently' signals action in progress
(C) reviewed — Wrong: past tense conflicts with ongoing signal
(D) reviewing — Wrong: missing auxiliary 'is'
Key Takeaway: Whenever you spot currently or at present in Part 5, test for is/are + -ing first.
MICRO-CHECK (1 QUESTION)

Check Your Understanding

The marketing team ------- new client proposals at the moment, so please do not disturb them.

Correct answer: (B) is evaluating

Explanation: The time adverbial "at the moment" indicates an action in progress right now, requiring the present continuous. The collective subject "team" takes the singular verb "is evaluating".

Rationale: (A) is simple present, (C) is simple past, and (D) is a participle lacking the required auxiliary "is".
