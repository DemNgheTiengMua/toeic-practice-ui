Question 8: your essay

Some companies now let their staff choose which days they work from home. Do you think this is a good idea?

I agree that letting staff choose their own days at home is a good idea, for two reasons. The first is concentration. In my own team, the work that needs long stretches of quiet — writing a report, reviewing a design — is almost impossible in our office, where the phone rings every few minutes. On the two days I work at home I finish that kind of task in half the time. The second reason is that the choice itself matters. A fixed rule, such as every Wednesday at home, sounds fair but ignores how people actually work: a parent may need Monday, and someone sharing a small flat may want the office every day. Letting people decide week by week respects that difference, and in my experience it costs the company nothing. There is one condition. A team cannot work if nobody is ever in the same room, so the days people choose have to be visible to everyone, and the team needs at least one day together each week. With that in place, I believe the choice is better for both the staff and the company.
