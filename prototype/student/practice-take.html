Question 7 Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences Accuracy

The regional manager ------- the quarterly figures before the board meeting.

A review
B reviewing
C will review Your answer · correct
D to review
Explanation

The sentence lacks a main verb, so eliminate it reviewing and to review. The time marker "before the board meeting" refers to something that has not happened yet, so choose the future tense will review. Type review does not agree with the singular subject.

Question 7 Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences Incorrect

The regional manager ------- the quarterly figures before the board meeting.

A review
B reviewing Your answer
C will review Correct answer
D to review
Explanation

reviewing is a V-ing form and cannot stand alone as the main verb — the sentence would have no predicate. The blank needs a conjugated verb, and "before the board meeting" shows the action has not happened yet, so the answer is will review.

A common trap in Part 5: putting a V-ing form in the main verb position.
