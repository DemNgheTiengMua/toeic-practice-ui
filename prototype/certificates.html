TOEIC — Listening & Reading

The standard workplace English test. 200 multiple-choice questions, about 2 hours, scored 10–990. Practice speaking and writing separately with AI-graded tasks.

Listening — 45 minutes · 100 questions
  • Part 1 — Photographs (6)
  • Part 2 — Question-Response (25)
  • Part 3 — Conversations (39)
  • Part 4 — Talks (30)
Reading — 75 minutes · 100 questions
  • Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences (30)
  • Part 6 — Text Completion (16)
  • Part 7 — Reading Comprehension (54)
Score bands
A1 A2 B1 B2 C1
120 225 550 785 945

10–990 scale. Bands follow the standard CEFR mapping.

IELTS — Academic & General

The four-skill test for study, migration and work abroad. Scored in 0.5 steps from 0.0 to 9.0, with a separate band for each skill.

Listening — about 30 minutes · 40 questions

Four recordings, from everyday conversation to academic lecture.

Reading — 60 minutes · 40 questions

Three long passages with a mix of question types.

Writing — 60 minutes · 2 tasks

Describe a chart (Task 1, 150 words) and write an essay (Task 2, 250 words). AI-graded against the public rubric.

Speaking — 11–14 minutes · 3 parts

Interview, cue-card talk and discussion. Recorded and AI-graded.

Band scale 0.0–9.0. Most universities ask for 6.0–7.0; the placement test tells you where you stand today.

VSTEP — the Vietnamese national test

The national English proficiency test used by Vietnamese universities and employers. Four skills, reported on a 0–10 scale mapped to CEFR levels A2 to C1.

Listening — about 40 minutes

Three parts, played once, with conversations and talks.

Reading — 60 minutes

Four passages, 40 multiple-choice questions.

Writing — 60 minutes

A letter or email, then an essay. AI-graded.

Speaking — about 12 minutes

Three parts: social interaction, description and discussion. Recorded and AI-graded.

Levels A2 (4.0) to C1 (8.5+). Level B1 (5.5–6.0) is the common graduation requirement.
