# Simplify Exam Authoring, Placement, Scoring, and Learning Path

Status: accepted

We are replacing the six-state item pool, four automated ingestion gates, blind stratified audit queue, two-stage adaptive placement draw, parallel CEFR scoring/cell-mastery engine, sliding-window path synthesizer, and `DerivedFactOwners` writer-guard triggers with a flat, deterministic model:

1. **Direct Exam & Flat Question/Lesson Bank + Streamlined Certificate Wizard (Lean 2-Certificate Seed)**: Exams (`mock`, `placement`, `speaking_practice`, `writing_practice`) have two states (`draft`, `published`) and are authored or imported in one step without ingestion gates or blind audit queues. Questions preserve rich render formats (`mcq`, `mcq_3`, `multi_select`, `inline_completion`, `dropdown`, `drag_drop`, `free_response`) and carry multi-target band tags (one or more band codes, or `ALL` for general foundational knowledge). Creating or editing a certificate via the `Certificate Wizard` uses a 2-skill (`Listening & Reading`) or 4-skill (`Listening, Reading, Speaking, Writing`) template with 3-field sections (`Code`, `Name`, `ExpectedQuestionCount` + automatic modality defaults), automatic linear `ScoreScales` generation, `ScoreBands`, and flat `GradingCriteria` (eliminating the manual Score Conversion matrix tab, Rubric Studio 7-band ladder, anchor essays, and `GradingRubricLocks`). Demo seed data is trimmed strictly to **1 two-skill certificate (`TOEIC` Listening & Reading)**, **1 four-skill certificate (`IELTS`)**, and **1 universal Placement Exam**, purging all redundant multi-certificate seed noise (`VSTEP`, `TOEIC_CAT`, `TOEIC_SW`).
2. **Single Universal Diagnostic Placement (2-Skill or 4-Skill Scope) & Unified Scoring**: Placement uses **one single universal diagnostic exam** across the platform (`purpose = placement`) sitting through `AttemptEngine`. Learners choose only whether to test **2 skills (`listening_and_reading`, instant objective grading)** or **4 skills (`all_skills`, including AI-graded Speaking/Writing)** before starting. Once graded, the placement score maps directly to the `ScoreBands` of whichever certificate track the learner selects next.
3. **Guided Learner OOBE with Post-Placement Certificate Selection, Soft-Lock & Temporary Skip**: Learners who have not completed placement and goal setup enter a step-by-step Out-Of-Box Experience: **(1) Sit Universal Placement (`2 skills` or `4 skills`) -> (2) Wait for AI Grading (if 4 skills) & View Diagnostic Level -> (3) Choose Certificate Track (`TOEIC` 2-skill or `IELTS` 4-skill), Target Band & Study Time (`GoalFeasibility`) -> (4) Generate 5-Credit Learning Path**. Normal study navigation is hidden during OOBE unless the learner clicks a temporary session-only skip (`Explore for now`) to browse Mentors/Wallet, in which case a persistent top banner leads back to OOBE and gated pages (`Learning Path`, `Practice`, `Weakness Analysis`) render guided soft-lock empty states.

4. **Paid 4-Week Deterministic Learning Path (5 Credits) & AI-Graded Productive Practice**: Generating a Learning Path charges 5 credits (`learning_path` ledger reason, enforcing "Block before you charge"), validates time feasibility (`GoalFeasibilityCalculator`), and builds a 4-week sequential plan matching the learner's `CurrentBand -> TargetBand` and `ALL` general-knowledge tags. Writing and Speaking practice steps include structured guides/lessons and full rubric-based AI grading (`Claude` + `SpeechSuper`) rather than ungraded drills.
5. **Stateless Practice Unlocked by Path Completion vs. Resumable Exam Attempts**: Free Practice is unlocked strictly for topics/sections the learner has already completed (`done`) in their Learning Path, making the 5-credit Learning Path the primary progression spine. Session resume (`in_progress` state tracking, countdown recovery, and optimistic answer queue sync) is restricted strictly to **Mock Exam** and **Placement Exam** sittings (`ExamAttempts`). Practice drills do not track `in_progress` or `abandoned` resume states; learners navigate unlocked completed topics vs. locked upcoming topics directly via the HeroUI v3 catalog UI, and only finished practice results are persisted.
6. **Direct Rubric-Grounded LLM Grading & Fact-Grounded AI Insights**: `ClaudeAiGrader` and `SpeechSuperAiGrader` grade directly against flat section `GradingCriteria` (`Name`, `Description`, `MaxScore`, `Weight`), the task prompt/guide, and the learner's Certificate `CurrentBand -> TargetBand` context without requiring 7-band `GradingCriterionDescriptors` ladders or `GradingCriterionAnchors`, returning per-criterion feedback, inline `ErrorSpans` (`originalSnippet`, `correctedSnippet`, `explanationVi`, `category`), `PriorityFix`, and `ModelRewrite`. `ClaudeAiInsightGenerator` receives deterministic attempt/practice accuracy facts (Certificate Band, per-skill scaled scores, weakest/strongest topics, and top wrong-answer fingerprints) without the legacy CEFR `InsightNarrativeGuard` rejection filter, returning structured Vietnamese/bilingual narratives and prioritized study recommendations (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`).
